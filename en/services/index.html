Innovation commercialisation

Find a route from technology to a product with real customers. We help you choose a commercialisation path, develop a business model and prepare a pilot project.

When to use this service

  • You have a research result or a technology and need a route to customers.
  • You are considering licensing, an industry partnership or a spin-off.
  • You want a pilot that tests the value of your solution in practice.

What you receive

  • A recommendation on the priority target market and the route to commercialisation.
  • A detailed business model and recommendations for your business plan.
  • A pilot validation plan and requirements for partners.
01

We define the opportunity

We review the technology, the available know-how, the team’s expectations and the starting point.

02

We choose a route

We compare potential applications and business models with customer needs.

03

We prepare for implementation

We agree on the partners, pilot validation activities and decision milestones needed.

Market potential validation

Base your next development decision on reliable evidence. We assess your solution’s readiness, customer needs, the market, the competition and key risks.

When to use this service

  • You need to understand who will use your solution and why.
  • You want to test technical and commercial assumptions before development or investment.
  • You need an independent assessment of innovation potential and risk.

What you receive

  • An assessment of technical and commercial readiness.
  • An analysis of the market, the competition and priority customer segments.
  • A list of key risks and a plan for assessing them.
01

We clarify the question

We define the decision the analysis should support and the assumptions to test.

02

We assess the evidence

We combine research with expert assessment and, where required by the brief, validation with users.

03

We recommend a way forward

We evaluate the findings and recommend the next step, including the conditions for putting it into practice.

Funding and grant support

We help you structure your project idea, build a consortium, prepare your proposal or improve it through an expert review. We have experience with national funding calls and European funding opportunities for deep-tech innovation, including Horizon Europe calls and support through the European Innovation Council (EIC) and the European Institute of Innovation and Technology (EIT).

When to use this service

  • You need funding for development, pilot validation or market entry.
  • You need to build a consortium of project partners and turn your idea into a high-quality project proposal.
  • You want an expert proposal review or an investment readiness assessment.

What you receive

  • An overview of relevant funding options and a recommended funding strategy.
  • A structure for your project and consortium, including a budget and milestones.
  • A competitive project proposal within the agreed scope for national or European grant programmes.
01

We assess your idea

We clarify the goals, readiness, budget and requirements of suitable funding instruments.

02

We develop the project

We develop the activities, identify the partners needed and define the outputs and schedule.

03

We strengthen the proposal

We coordinate the agreed preparation work and expert review before submission.

Venture building and startup acceleration

Turn technological potential into a viable business. We help build teams, validate business models, connect with initial customers and prepare for investment.

When to use this service

  • You are starting a technology company or a research spin-off.
  • You need to strengthen your commercial skills and validate your solution with customers.
  • You are looking for funding to accelerate technology development and are preparing your first pilot.

What you receive

  • An assessment of your team and product, including technical and commercial readiness.
  • Connections with experienced entrepreneurs who can help you lead your company.
  • Preparation for customer pilots, grant funding and investment.
01

We get to know the team

We assess the idea, the team’s capabilities and motivation, and the results achieved so far.

02

We tailor the support

We select a suitable combination of mentoring, funding sources, validation and partnerships.

03

We review progress

We use milestones and feedback from customers, partners and investors to assess progress.

Analysis and decision support

Base your decisions on reliable evidence. We analyse technology trends, markets and innovation ecosystems using transparent methods and traceable sources, and provide recommendations for your next steps.

When to use this service

  • You need to map a technology sector, a market or the capabilities of companies and research teams.
  • You are deciding on development priorities, assessing a new opportunity or planning innovation support.
  • You need evidence for a strategy, a programme evaluation or a project impact assessment.

What you receive

  • An analytical report setting out the question, the methodology, the sources and the limitations.
  • A dataset, stakeholder map or comparison of options tailored to the brief.
  • A summary of findings, scenarios and recommendations for decision-making.
01

We define the brief

We define the decision to be supported, the scope, the assessment criteria and an appropriate methodology.

02

We verify and analyse the data

We verify the sources, process the data and assess its quality and uncertainties.

03

We develop recommendations

We turn the results into clear findings and evidence for the next step.
