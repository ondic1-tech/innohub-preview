Innovation commercialisation

Find a route from technology to a product with real customers. We help you choose a commercialisation path, develop a business model and prepare a pilot application.

When to use this service

  • You have a research result or technology and need a route to customers.
  • You are considering licensing, an industry partnership or a spin-off.
  • You want a pilot that tests the value of your solution in practice.

What you receive

  • A proposed priority market and commercialisation route.
  • A developed business model and recommendations for your business plan.
  • A pilot validation plan and requirements for partners.
01

Define the opportunity

Review the technology, available know-how, team expectations and starting point.

02

Choose a route

Compare potential applications and business models with customer needs.

03

Prepare delivery

Agree the partners, pilot validation and decision milestones needed.

We agree the specific scope, outputs and funding approach after an initial assessment of your idea.

Technology and market validation

Base your next development decision on reliable evidence. We assess solution readiness, customer needs, the market, competition and key risks.

When to use this service

  • You need to understand who will use your solution and why.
  • You want to test technical and commercial assumptions before development or investment.
  • You need an independent assessment of innovation potential and risk.

What you receive

  • An assessment of technology and commercial readiness.
  • An analysis of the market, competition and priority customer segments.
  • Key risks and a plan to test them.
01

Clarify the question

Define the decision the analysis should support and the assumptions to test.

02

Assess the evidence

Combine research with expert assessment and, where agreed, user validation.

03

Recommend a course

Evaluate the findings and recommend the next step and its conditions.

We agree the specific scope, outputs and funding approach after an initial assessment of your idea.

Funding and projects

Connect your innovation development plan with suitable funding sources. We help structure project ideas, build a consortium and prepare or review a proposal.

When to use this service

  • You need funding for development, pilot validation or market entry.
  • You need partners and a proposal built around your project idea.
  • You want an expert proposal review or an investment readiness assessment.

What you receive

  • Relevant funding instruments and a recommended funding approach.
  • A project structure, consortium, budget and milestones.
  • A prepared or reviewed proposal within the agreed scope.
01

Assess the idea

Clarify goals, readiness, budget and the requirements of suitable instruments.

02

Develop the project

Plan activities, partners, outputs and a delivery schedule.

03

Strengthen the proposal

Coordinate the agreed preparation and expert review before submission.

For EU framework programmes, we draw on NICER expertise. Instrument suitability, call availability and funding conditions are assessed for each project.

Startups and spin-offs

Turn technological potential into a viable business. We support team formation, business model validation, early customers and investment readiness.

When to use this service

  • You are starting a technology company or a research spin-off.
  • You need business skills and customer validation.
  • You are preparing an initial pilot, a reference customer or investor discussions.

What you receive

  • An assessment of the team, product and technical and commercial readiness.
  • A development plan with milestones and a recommended support format.
  • Preparation for customer pilots and discussions with partners or investors.
01

Meet the team

Assess the concept, skills, motivation and progress so far.

02

Agree the support

Choose a relevant combination of mentoring, validation and partnerships.

03

Review progress

Use milestones and feedback from customers, partners and investors.

Support scope and duration follow an assessment of the case. Software and AI teams use faster validation cycles; hardware teams need coordinated development, testing and funding plans.

Analysis and decision support

Base your decisions on reliable evidence. We analyse technology trends, markets and innovation ecosystems using transparent methods, traceable sources and recommendations for your next steps.

When to use this service

  • You need to map a technology sector, market or the capabilities of companies and research teams.
  • You are deciding on development priorities, a new opportunity or innovation support.
  • You need evidence for a strategy, programme evaluation or project impact assessment.

What you receive

  • An analytical report defining the question, methodology, sources and limitations.
  • A dataset, stakeholder map or comparison of options tailored to the brief.
  • A summary of findings, scenarios and recommendations for decision-making.
01

Define the brief

Agree the decision question, scope, assessment criteria and appropriate methodology.

02

Verify and analyse the data

Check sources, process the data and assess quality and uncertainty.

03

Develop recommendations

Turn results into clear findings and evidence for the next step.

We select methods to match the brief and available data. Sources, assumptions and limitations are documented so findings can be assessed and the evidence reused.
