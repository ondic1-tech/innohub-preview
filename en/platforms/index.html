Our platforms

We connect you with space technologies, leading research and cybersecurity expertise. Discover the platforms we contribute to.

Space technologies

01

ESA Technology Broker

We help transfer space technologies into terrestrial applications and identify opportunities for technologies to be used in space.

Our role

In Czechia, we provide technology scouting, partner searches and support for pilot applications, including ESA Spark Funding.

Deep tech and laser technologies

02

ELIgnite

Connecting the ELI ERIC infrastructure with companies and research teams to validate and develop breakthrough technologies.

Our role

As a project partner, we manage cascade funding and the selection of supported teams. We also contribute to business mentoring.

More about ELIgnite↗ (opens in a new window)

Cybersecurity and digitalisation

03

Cybersecurity Innovation Hub

A European Digital Innovation Hub supporting cybersecurity, testing and skills development for companies and public organisations.

Our role

As a member of the CIH II consortium, we connect innovation support with the hub’s expertise and opportunities for secure digitalisation.

Industry and research infrastructures

04

Industry Liaison Officer

A contact point connecting Czech companies with major research infrastructures and their supplier opportunities.

Our role

We monitor the needs of research infrastructures, identify opportunities for Czech industry and help establish collaboration.
