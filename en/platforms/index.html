Our platforms

We connect you with space technologies, leading research and cybersecurity expertise. Discover the platforms we contribute to and through which we support applications in advanced technology sectors.

Space technologies

ESA Technology Broker

Transfer of space technologies into terrestrial applications and support for Czech innovative companies’ involvement in space activities.

Our role

In Czechia, we provide technology scouting and support technological collaboration and know-how transfer, including funding for pilot applications through ESA Spark Funding.

ESA Technology Broker website↗ (opens in a new window)

Laser technologies and Deep tech

ELIgnite

Connecting the ELI ERIC infrastructure with companies and research teams to validate and develop breakthrough technologies.

Our role

As a project partner, we coordinate the selection and funding of pilot laser applications and collaborate on their further development.

More about ELIgnite↗ (opens in a new window)

Cybersecurity and digitalisation

Cybersecurity Innovation Hub

A European Digital Innovation Hub supporting cybersecurity, testing and skills development for companies and public organisations.

Our role

As a member of the CIH II consortium, we connect innovation support with the hub’s expertise and opportunities for secure digitalisation.

Cybersecurity Innovation Hub website↗ (opens in a new window)

Industry and research infrastructures

Industry Liaison Officer

A contact point connecting Czech companies with major research infrastructures and their supplier opportunities.

Our role

We monitor the needs of research infrastructures, identify opportunities for Czech industry and help establish collaboration.
