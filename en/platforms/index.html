Startup creation and growth

We support the creation and growth of technology companies. Commercialization Reactor brings researchers and entrepreneurs together to establish spin-offs. TC Runway helps startups validate their markets, acquire customers and prepare for further growth.

Commercialization Reactor

Venture building and spin-off creation

This international programme brings researchers and entrepreneurs together to create deep tech spin-offs. Researchers continue developing their technology and can pursue their academic careers alongside the venture.

Our role

As the national partner for Czechia, we help research teams assess whether their technology is suitable, prepare for the programme and coordinate with their home institution.

Project manager
Ondřej Šimeksimek@tc.cz
