TOSEDA × BD Sensors

ESA Technology BrokerCompleted collaboration

TOSEDA offered its materials through the ESA Technology Broker programme, leading to collaboration with the pressure sensor manufacturer BD Sensors. A polymer system was developed to encapsulate electronic components. The published case study documents the completed collaboration.

What the case demonstrates

Matching a technology offer to a specific industrial need.
