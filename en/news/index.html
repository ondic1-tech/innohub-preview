TC Business Innovation Hub starts its activities

18 November 2026

TC Business Innovation Hub starts its activities as a specialised team within Technology Centre Prague. It brings together support for research commercialisation, technology businesses and the preparation of innovation projects.

▶Find out more

TC Business Innovation Hub (INNOHUB) builds on the experience and partnerships of TC Prague’s Business Development Department. Under a shared brand, it connects expertise and programmes that help turn research results and technology ideas into practical applications.

Researchers, start-ups, spin-offs and established companies can receive support from assessing technology and market potential through finding partners and funding to preparing pilot validation and a business model. Each collaboration responds to a specific need and an agreed outcome.

INNOHUB connects the work of TC Runway, ESA Technology Broker, ELIgnite, Cybersecurity Innovation Hub, ILO and Commercialization Reactor. Clients can draw on complementary support across sectors and stages of innovation.

ELIgnite project launches

1 October 2026

ELIgnite connects ELI’s advanced laser research infrastructure with the needs of companies and research teams. TC Prague will help select promising technologies and prepare them for market applications.

▶Find out more

The European ELIgnite project starts on 1 October 2026. Led by ELI ERIC, the consortium connects research infrastructure, technology companies and partners experienced in commercialisation and deep-tech venture building.

The project aims to help development teams validate new technologies using ELI’s facilities and expertise and bring them closer to industrial needs. Technical validation will be combined with support for business readiness and identifying practical applications.

Technology Centre Prague will coordinate the selection of supported projects and involve experts in their assessment. This role builds on our experience in technology evaluation, market analysis and support for researchers and start-ups.

Commercialization Reactor Ignition Event

1 July 2026

The autumn Commercialization Reactor Ignition Event is confirmed for 13–14 November 2026. The two-day online event will connect researchers with entrepreneurs and create opportunities for new deep-tech spin-offs.

▶Find out more

Save 13–14 November 2026. The Ignition Event gives research teams an opportunity to present their technologies to people interested in building new businesses. Participants look for suitable partners and explore whether they can form a strong entrepreneurial team.

The programme is aimed particularly at technologies validated in laboratory conditions, typically from TRL 4. Researchers can continue developing the scientific side of their work, while an entrepreneurial partner contributes business and company-building expertise.

Technology Centre Prague is Commercialization Reactor’s national partner for Czechia. We help researchers assess whether their technology fits the programme, prepare for participation and work with their research institution. We recommend starting preparations well ahead of the November event.

CIH II project launches

1 January 2026

Cybersecurity Innovation Hub enters its second phase on 1 January 2026. CIH II supports secure digital transformation for businesses and public organisations. TC Prague contributes expertise in innovation, commercialisation and funding.

▶Find out more

Cybersecurity Innovation Hub II (CIH II) starts on 1 January 2026. The follow-on project develops the work of a European Digital Innovation Hub focused on cybersecurity and the secure use of digital technologies.

It helps small and medium-sized businesses and public organisations navigate digital transformation. Its services connect testing before investment, training and skills development, support in finding investment opportunities and access to expert partners.

Technology Centre Prague contributes its experience in innovation development, assessing market applications and identifying appropriate funding. Participation in CIH II complements INNOHUB’s support for technology businesses and research teams.
