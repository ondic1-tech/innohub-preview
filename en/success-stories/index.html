How TC Prague helps companies apply technologies, build partnerships and grow.

Apis Innovation: from space to beehives

27 November 2024

Space insulation materials helped improve a solar-heated beehive. TC Prague connected Apis Innovation with technologies and their providers.

▶Find out more

Thermosolar Hive uses solar heat against the Varroa mite. The company needed materials for new hive designs.

TC Prague reviewed ESA patents, recommended insulation materials including aerogels and facilitated cooperation with technology providers.

The result was a lighter, better-insulated hive for different climates. The project also placed second in ESA’s Get Space Inspired competition.

Dronetag: safer drone identification

25 November 2024

Satellite communications expertise found a new use in drone identification. TC Prague supported the preparation of the Dronetag RIDER project.

▶Find out more

Dronetag and the Czech Technical University applied satellite communications know-how to more reliable drone identification and protection against signal spoofing.

TC Prague introduced ESA Spark Funding and advised on eligibility, project roles and the business plan.

ESA accepted the project for funding, connecting space expertise with drone safety needs.

Argotech: from eight days to six hours

19 June 2023

Software used in space applications accelerated optical component simulations. TC Prague connected Argotech with technology partner CST.

▶Find out more

Argotech needed faster, more accurate modelling for high-speed optical communications. Its calculations took up to eight days.

TC Prague introduced CST and its electromagnetic simulation tools, used in space applications.

Testing reduced simulation time to six hours. Argotech purchased the software and integrated it into optical component development.

Iterait: bringing AI closer to customers

9 April 2021

TC Prague helped Iterait explore commercial applications for its AI technologies, protect intellectual property and prepare for growth.

▶Find out more

Iterait developed computer vision for healthcare, manufacturing and marketing. It needed customers and a business strategy for its Vividi camera.

TC Prague advised on intellectual property, identified funding opportunities and involved the company in Scale-up your business mentoring.

At publication, Iterait was discussing a German pilot. Vividi received an honourable mention in the 2020 Vizionáři competition.

Vitadio: from mentoring to investment

1 June 2020

Investor preparation helped Vitadio secure funding for digital diabetes support. The company took part in TC Prague’s mentoring programme.

▶Find out more

Vitadio needed funding to develop an online programme supporting patients between medical appointments.

TC Prague involved the company in Scale-up your business and prepared it for an investor presentation in October 2019, where it met a future investor.

In March 2020, Vitadio secured CZK 16 million from an angel investor and Nation 1 for further development.
