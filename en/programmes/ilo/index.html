Industry and research infrastructure

ILO

The Industrial Liaison Office connects Czech industry with major research infrastructures. We help companies understand technology needs, partnerships and supply opportunities.

What support it offers

  • Map infrastructure needs and relevant company capabilities.
  • Connect companies with research and industry partners.
  • Understand supply opportunities and requirements.

Who the programme is for

For technology companies with distinctive products, know-how or services, and facilities seeking industry partners and users.
