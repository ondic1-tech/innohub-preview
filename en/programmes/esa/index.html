Space technology and applications

ESA Technology Broker

ESA Technology Broker Czech Republic connects technology and know-how between space and terrestrial applications. The programme is operated in Czechia by Technology Centre Prague.

What support it offers

  • Identify technology, new applications and suitable partners.
  • Assess technical feasibility and commercial potential.
  • Prepare a pilot concept and assess suitability for ESA Spark Funding.

Who the programme is for

For companies, startups and research teams applying space know-how on Earth or adapting terrestrial technology for space.

Spark Funding has its own eligibility rules and deadlines. Check the programme website for current calls and documentation, and discuss suitability before applying.
