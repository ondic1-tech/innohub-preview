Cybersecurity and innovation

Cybersecurity Innovation Hub

The Cybersecurity Innovation Hub is a specialised platform within INNOHUB’s portfolio that connects innovation with cybersecurity. We help define clients’ needs and identify the right expertise and ways to collaborate.

What support it offers

  • Support in defining the need for a technology solution and its context.
  • Connections with expert partners suited to your brief.
  • Links between technology validation and the development of your innovation.

Who the programme is for

For companies and research teams whose innovations address cybersecurity or need to account for security, data and software reliability.
