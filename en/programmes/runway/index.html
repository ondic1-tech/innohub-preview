Incubation and acceleration

TC Runway

TC Runway is a platform run by the Technology Centre Prague to incubate, accelerate and develop technology startups and spin-offs. We tailor support to the innovation’s potential, the team’s needs and the venture’s stage of development.

What support it offers

  • An assessment of technical and commercial readiness.
  • Mentoring in business model and team development.
  • Validation with customers and preparation for partnerships or investment.

Who the programme is for

For teams that are developing their own technological innovation and need to validate its commercial potential or take their company to the next stage.
