Incubation and acceleration

TC Runway

Technology Centre Prague’s platform for incubation, acceleration and development of technology startups and spin-offs. Support follows an assessment of the innovation, team and development stage.

What support it offers

  • Technology and commercial readiness assessment.
  • Business model and team development mentoring.
  • Customer validation and preparation for partnerships or investment.

Who the programme is for

For teams developing a technology innovation that need to validate commercial potential or move their business forward.
