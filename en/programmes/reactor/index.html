Science and entrepreneurship

Commercialization Reactor

An international programme brings scientific and entrepreneurial talent together to build deep-tech spin-offs. Technology Centre Prague is the national partner for Czechia.

What support it offers

  • Assess technology suitability for the programme.
  • Support Czech research teams in preparing for programme stages.
  • Connect with entrepreneurial talent and coordinate with the home institution.

Who the programme is for

For researchers with technology validated at least in a laboratory (TRL 4), seeking to assess commercial potential and find an entrepreneurial partner.

Write an emailProgramme website

The scientific role can continue alongside an academic career. Participation, rights to research results and institutional consent need to be agreed for each case.
